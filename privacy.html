Privacy

Last updated November 16, 2026

This page covers the Omni Athletics website, the client portal and the Omni iPhone app. Omni Athletics is an online coaching business based in Washington, USA.

What we collect

  • Account details you give us: your name, email, and phone number if you add it.
  • Coaching details you enter: your goal, weight and check ins, energy and sleep ratings, training sessions and notes, and progress photos if you upload them in the client portal.
  • Messages between you and your coach.
  • Payment records your coach enters, such as an amount, a due date and how you paid. We do not collect or store card or bank numbers.

How we use it

Only to run your coaching: to show you your progress, let your coach plan your training, and keep track of what is paid. We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who can see it

You and your Omni coach. Your information is stored with our hosting and database provider (Supabase) in the United States, and the app and portal were built for Omni by Suubi Media, who may access the system to maintain it.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time. In the iPhone app or the client portal, open your account (your initials at the top right) and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, check ins, sessions, messages and payment records. You can also ask your coach to delete it for you.

Children

If you are under 18, use Omni only with a parent or guardian who has agreed to it.

Health note

Omni is coaching, not medical care. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training or nutrition plan.

Contact

Questions about privacy or your data: message Omni on Instagram at @oa.omni.athletics or book a call with David.
