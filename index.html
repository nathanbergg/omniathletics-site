FAQ

Questions.

Still unsure which path fits? Book a free consultation and ask.

Health or Performance?

Omni Health is for adults training for strength, energy and long term wellness. Omni Performance is for athletes training for their sport, from high school to pro. If you are both, start with your main goal and your coach will build around the rest.

Is coaching online?

Mostly. Programs, video demos, check ins and messages with your coach all run through the Everfit app, so you can train at home, in a gym or on the field. In home training is available too. Want us to come to you? Reach out and we can see what works.

What equipment do I need?

Each program is built around the equipment you actually have, from a home setup to a full gym.

Which membership is right for me?

Essentials is a prebuilt program with monthly check ins. Plus adds bi weekly check ins plus macro and nutrition guidance. Premium is fully individualized training and nutrition with weekly check ins. Not sure? That is what the free consultation is for.

How is athlete training priced?

Individual and team training are quoted, not listed. After a conversation about sport, training frequency, schedule, season length and number of athletes, you get a written scope and price before you decide. No payment is needed to ask.

How do I start?

Book your free consultation. It is a short conversation about your goals, experience and schedule. From there we recommend a training rhythm and membership on Health, or a coaching scope and quote on Performance.

What am I committing to?

Memberships run 3, 6 or 12 months, billed at the monthly rate for the term you choose. Your coach walks through the terms before you enroll, so there are no surprises.

Is Performance training one on one?

It depends on the athlete or team and what we agree on together. Some athletes train fully individualized, teams train on a program built for the roster. Scope is spelled out in your quote.
